This policy covers the fees you pay for an AcadeMe cohort programme, such as DevOps Engineer or AI Engineering. It's written to be read in two minutes. If something here is unclear, email us before you pay and we'll explain.

## The short version

- **Changed your mind? Full refund until week 2 starts.** No reason needed, no fees taken off.
- **Can't make this cohort? Move to a later one for free**, any time before week 3 starts.
- **After week 2 starts, fees aren't refundable**, except in the cases listed under "When we'll always refund".
- **If we cancel or postpone a cohort**, you choose between a full refund and a free move to another cohort.

## 1. Full refund until week 2 starts

Every cohort runs for six weeks. Week 1 starts on the cohort's start date, shown on the programme page and in your dashboard.

You can cancel for a **full refund** up to **23:59 UAE time (GMT+4) on the last day of week 1**, which is the day before week 2 starts. That includes cancelling before the cohort has started at all.

It doesn't matter how much of week 1 you attended or which materials you opened. You get back everything you paid.

**Example.** Your cohort starts Monday 5 October. Week 1 runs 5 to 11 October. You can cancel for a full refund until 23:59 on Sunday 11 October. From Monday 12 October (week 2), the fee is no longer refundable.

## 2. Moving to a later cohort

Life happens. You can move your place to a later cohort of the same programme **once, for free**, as long as you ask **before week 3 of your current cohort starts**.

- The new cohort must start within **[12] months** of your original start date.
- You keep the price you paid, even if the programme costs more by then.
- Your work carries over: completed lessons, submitted labs and graded assignments stay on your record.
- After a move, section 1 applies to the new cohort's dates. If you cancel before the new cohort's week 2 starts, you get a full refund.
- A second move is up to us and depends on space.

If the programme price has gone down by the time you move, we don't refund the difference.

## 3. After week 2 starts

From the start of week 2, the programme fee is not refundable and we don't give partial refunds for:

- classes, labs or assignments you missed
- leaving the programme part way through
- not finishing in time for the certificate

This is because each cohort is small and your place can't be offered to someone else once it's under way. You keep access to the recordings and materials of the weeks you were enrolled for [for 12 months after the cohort ends].

## 4. When we'll always refund

Whatever the date, you get a **full refund** if:

- **We cancel the cohort**, or move its start date by more than [14] days, and you don't want a place on another cohort.
- **We don't deliver what we promised.** For example, most live classes don't happen and aren't rescheduled, or the curriculum is substantially different from what the programme page described when you paid. Here we may offer a partial refund instead if only part of the programme was affected.
- **You were charged twice or charged the wrong amount.** We refund the extra straight away.

If a serious medical or family situation stops you continuing after week 2, email us. We'll look at each case individually and usually offer a free move to a later cohort. We may offer a partial refund where a move doesn't make sense.

## 5. If we remove you from a cohort

If we remove you for a serious breach of the [community guidelines](#) or the [terms of use](#) (for example harassment, sharing other students' personal data, or submitting someone else's work as yours), you won't get a refund for weeks 2 onwards. If this happens during week 1, section 1 still applies and you get a full refund.

## 6. How to ask for a refund or a move

Email **[refunds@academe.example]** from the email address on your account, with:

- your full name
- the programme and cohort, for example "DevOps Engineer, cohort #01"
- whether you want a refund or a move (and which cohort you'd like to move to)

The time your email reaches us is what counts for the deadlines above. We'll confirm we've received it within **2 working days**.

## 7. How you get your money back

- Refunds go back to the **original payment method** and in the **original currency (USD)**.
- We approve refunds within **5 working days** of your request.
- After that it usually takes **5 to 10 working days** for the money to show up, depending on your bank or card provider. We don't control that part.
- We don't deduct processing or admin fees from a refund.
- If you paid in instalments, we refund what you've paid so far and cancel any remaining payments. [Delete this line if instalments aren't offered.]
- If your employer or a sponsor paid, we refund whoever paid. Let us know if the payment details have changed.

Your access to the cohort ends when the refund is approved. Any certificate issued for that cohort is revoked.

## 8. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits the rights you have under UAE law, including Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 on Consumer Protection. If the law gives you more than this policy does, the law wins.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled a refund request, reply to our email and ask for the decision to be reviewed by [Rakan Matouq, founder]. You can also contact the UAE Ministry of Economy and Tourism's consumer protection service.
